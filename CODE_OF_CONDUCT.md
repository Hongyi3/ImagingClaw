# Code of Conduct

ClawImaging is a professional, research-focused open-source project. Everyone participating in the
project is expected to contribute to an environment that is respectful, constructive, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific in technical disagreement
- Critique ideas, not people
- Help newcomers orient themselves
- Prefer evidence, experiments, and documentation over status or volume
- Acknowledge uncertainty honestly

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks
- Demeaning comments about identity, background, or experience
- Deliberate misrepresentation of scientific results
- Repeatedly ignoring project rules after feedback
- Publishing private information without permission

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject content that violates these expectations, and may restrict
participation when necessary to protect the project and its community.
